## 1. Executive Summary

1. **The market is real, large, and compounding.** Brazil's agriculture-drone market generated **~US$ 77M in 2024–25 revenue** and is forecast to reach **US$ 150–292M by 2030–31** (CAGR estimates range 17.7%–25.1% depending on scope). The operating fleet exploded from ~3,000 drones (2021) to ~35,000 (2025), with the ag-aviation union Sindag projecting **90,000+ drones by 2026**. Growth is not the question; *who captures the service layer* is.
2. **Our internal claim "no relevant competitor packages mapping + AI + portal" is now FALSE in our core territory.** ARPAC operates 8 bases in **São Paulo, Goiás, and Minas Gerais treating almost exclusively sugarcane** — exactly our GO/SP sugarcane prospect list — with 14 DJI drones, ~70,000 ha of accumulated 2025 service, ~R$ 7M revenue growing 55% with positive EBITDA, plus **continuous photogrammetry operations in 4 hubs** and its own pilot-training pipeline. XMobots (Embraer-backed, US$ 28M raised) claims **~60% share of precision-ag services to the sugar-energy industry** via its "Cana Solution." The moat statement in `MARKET_INTELLIGENCE.md` v1.0 must be corrected.
3. **Mapping prices are being commoditized from below.** Published Brazilian benchmarks put RGB mapping at **R$ 40–60/ha above 100 ha** (falling with scale), while free Sentinel-2/SATVeg satellite NDVI and OEM platforms (John Deere Operations Center with Starlink connectivity, Climate FieldView) erode the low end. Our Essencial anchor (R$ 35/ha) is safe; our value capture must migrate up-stack to multi-product packages, recurring programs, and Enterprise audit products.
4. **Our spraying (DaaS) anchor of R$ 150/ha is priced for specialty crops, not row crops.** Market rates for large-scale soy/corn spraying run **R$ 20–80/ha**; only horticulture/specialty reaches R$ 100–250/ha. Sugarcane sits in between. The Deal Desk band (R$ 120–180) will lose every competitive row-crop deal it touches and needs crop-segmented bands.
5. **Carbon MRV is the open flank — and it just got a regulatory engine.** Law 15.042/2024 created Brazil's regulated carbon market (SBCE): ~5,000 industrial companies will face decarbonization targets and mandatory monitoring plans and emission reports in the phase-in. Meanwhile the incumbent voluntary-market leaders are wounded: Carbonext was linked to a REDD+ fraud investigation and **Verra suspended three Brazilian projects in 2025**. Rigorous, field-verified, drone-based MRV is a differentiation window that plays directly to our strengths.
6. **Net assessment:** Agrostech's defensible position is **not** "drone mapping" (contested, commoditizing) but the **bundled data-service layer for 500–50,000 ha producers** — multi-product per-flight packaging, WhatsApp-native sales/delivery, production-audit reports bankable for rural credit, and carbon-grade field data. Speed matters: ARPAC is one product decision away from bundling analytics onto its spraying base, and XMobots owns the usina relationships.

## 6. Threat Vector 3 — Satellite Substitution

- **Free tier:** Sentinel-2 NDVI/NDWI via free platforms; Embrapa's SATVeg now serves Sentinel-2 through the Brazil Data Cube with a redesigned interface ([Geopera — free satellite data for Brazil](https://geopera.com/blog/free-sources-satellite-data-brazil); [MDPI — NDVI + digital-ag tools in southern Brazil](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-7402/8/2/48)).
- **Paid tier:** EOS Crop Monitoring, PlanetScope (~3 m daily), used in Brazilian cover-crop/biomass research ([EOS](https://eos.com/products/crop-monitoring/satellite-images-for-agriculture/); [MDPI PlanetScope+Sentinel-1 study](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/16/15/2686)).
- **Structural limits of satellite (our defense):** cloud cover disrupts systematic monitoring through the crop cycle — a documented problem motivating radar workarounds ([MDPI Sentinel-1 study](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/11/12/1441)); 10 m-class resolution cannot do plant-level counts, planting-failure metering, or centimeter topography for drainage; and satellite outputs are not accepted as field-verified evidence for production audits or carbon field baselines **[EST]**.
- **Threat profile:** satellite kills the *standalone NDVI subscription* over time (our R$ 2.5–15k/month recurring line is exposed) but **cannot** kill 2–5 cm orthophoto, failure counts, DEM, or MRV ground-truthing. Bundle satellite *into* our recurring product (satellite between flights, drone for verification) rather than pretend it doesn't exist.

- **John Deere Operations Center:** 86,000+ connected machines globally, expanding hard in Brazil; **JDLink Boost (Starlink)** removes the connectivity barrier on farms where only ~34% of land has 4G/5G ([AgTech Navigator](https://www.agtechnavigator.com/Article/2025/06/12/john-deere-grows-in-brazil-with-help-of-agtech/)).
- **Climate FieldView (Bayer):** Brazil pilot cut pesticide use ~15% for soy farmers; integrates with Deere Ops Center ([Climate](https://climate.com/en-us/partners/john-deere-operations-center.html); [IJIRSET synthesis](https://www.ijirset.com/upload/2024/june/210_Synergy.pdf)).
- **Orbia** (Bayer-linked marketplace) already retails Horus Mappa credits — input distribution channels are becoming drone-analytics channels ([Orbia](https://www.orbia.ag/produto/65843/RA0693/0/creditos-mappa-software-agriculture-drone-analysis-horus-aeronaves-orbia)).
- **Threat profile:** MED-HIGH over 24 months. OEM platforms own the machinery data plane and the dealer relationship; when they bundle imagery, the standalone map gets squeezed. **Counter:** be the *field-data supplier* these platforms ingest (prescriptions exported in Deere/FieldView formats — zero-cost integration claim we can make today) and own what they don't: regulatory documents, carbon MRV, bank-grade audits.

### Landscape

| Player | Position | Vulnerability/Note |
|---|---|---|

| **Moss.Earth** | Tokenized carbon credits, LatAm retail/distribution | Trading/tokenization, not field MRV ([Causeartist](https://www.causeartist.com/carbon-credit-platforms/)) |
| Verra ecosystem | Standard-setter | **Suspended three Brazilian projects in 2025** after police investigation into land-grabbing/logging links ([Mongabay](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/01/in-brazil-planting-forests-for-carbon-credits-could-help-ecosystem-restoration/)) |

### Regulatory engine: Law 15.042/2024 (SBCE)

- Sanctioned Dec/2024; creates Brazil's cap-and-trade system. **~5,000 industrial companies** get decarbonization targets, tradable CBE quotas and CRVE verified-reduction certificates ([Senado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2024/12/12/sancionada-lei-que-regula-mercado-de-carbono-no-brasil); [XP](https://conteudos.xpi.com.br/esg/credito-carbono-mercado-brasil/)).
- Phase III (two years) requires operators to submit **monitoring plans and GHG emission/removal reports** — an MRV demand wave with fines up to 3–4% of gross revenue for non-compliance ([Rocha Cerqueira](https://rochacerqueira.com.br/mercado-de-carbono-lei15042-24/); [Demarest](https://www.demarest.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DEMAREST-Mercado-de-Carbono-no-Brasil-%E2%80%93-Lei-no-15.042.pdf)).
- Decreto 12.679/2025 + Resolução SFB 30/2025 open certification paths for forest projects on public/concession lands ([Rocha Cerqueira](https://rochacerqueira.com.br/mercado-de-carbono-lei15042-24/)).
- Primary agriculture is outside the capped sector but is the **supply side** for CRVEs (removals) — exactly where farm-scale, field-verified biomass data is scarce.

**Strategic read:** the incumbents' credibility crisis (fraud probes, Verra suspensions) + a new compliance market = the strongest whitespace in this report. Nobody owns "**audit-grade, drone-verified, farm-scale MRV in the Cerrado corridor**." Our KR4.x OKRs point here but with zero progress so far; competitors are consultancies and GIS shops without our field-data cost position. **[EST]** Window: 12–24 months before satellite-MRV platforms and Big-4-style consultancies verticalize downward.

1. **ARPAC bundles analytics onto its cana spraying base** before we lock our 93 GO/SP prospects (its photogrammetry hubs say it is already moving).
2. **Deal Desk sells DaaS spraying at R$ 120–180 into row crops** and burns credibility with exactly the large soy accounts (Bruno Luiz profile) we need.
3. **Standalone recurring NDVI eroded** by free satellite + OEM bundles → our R$ 2.5–15k/month line decays.
4. XMobots' enterprise lock-in of usinas closes the top of the cana market to us.
5. A credibility-scarred carbon market makes buyers slower to trust any new MRV vendor (headwind even in the whitespace).

### Top opportunities (ranked)
1. **SBCE-driven MRV wave** with wounded incumbents: audit-grade, drone-verified farm/forest MRV — aligns with OKR Obj. 4, currently 0%.
2. **Partner-pilot network arbitrage:** thousands of new DJI operators need demand; we own demand generation (WhatsApp SDR bot) — scale DaaS without capex while rivals buy fleets.
3. **Enterprise production audit** (no competitor found): bank/insurer/fundo-facing laudos as the anti-commodity flagship.
4. **Prescription-export integration** with Deere Ops Center / FieldView file formats — ride the OEM wave instead of fighting it.
5. **Small-area premium tier** (< 100 ha at R$ 60–90/ha with project minimum) — the published market charges more than our v1.0 bands assume.

### Moat restatement (replaces the v1.0 claim)
> Not "nobody bundles mapping + AI + portal" — ARPAC and XMobots partially do. The defensible moat is: **asset-light multi-product packaging (5 products per flight at +R$ 1.90/ha marginal cost), WhatsApp-native autonomous sales, season-over-season data custody, and audit/carbon-grade documentation** — a combination none of the identified competitors currently assembles, and which is cheapest to defend in the 500–50,000 ha producer segment.
