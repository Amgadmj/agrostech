| Risk | Leading indicator | Kill/contingency criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Soy/corn DaaS economics don't close | Rate cards > R$ 25/ha all-in | **KILL at day 90** (action 3.3): shelve row-crop spraying for 2026; cana/specialty only |
| Partner-pilot quality breaks SLA/QA | Rework > 3% or any safety incident | Freeze partner missions; revert to own-pilot capacity; retrain bench before resuming |
| Blitz under-converts | < 5 contracts from the 93 list by day 60 (action 2.7) | Re-tier assumptions, shift weight to soy volume + carbon accounts; flag Obj. 2 red |
| Carbon buyers stay frozen post-scandals | No MRV commitment by day 45 | Reposition pilots as "SBCE-readiness audits" (compliance framing, not credits); revisit at day 90 |
| MRR shortfall vs KR1.1 (CFO pessimistic case) | MRR < 50% of R$ 80k at day 60 | Mandatory Pacote-only quoting; pause all non-CAC marketing spend; CEO reviews cost base |
| Price war drags mapping to the floor | ≥ 2 deals lost at ≥ R$ 30/ha quotes | Hold floors (Bruno Luiz rule); escalate to board before any floor change — never quote below R$ 29 |
