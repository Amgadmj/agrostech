## Channels

| Channel | Evidence it works | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct to consultants/agronomists with large managed footprints** | Thales — independent cacao consultant covering ~30,000 ha in Bahia — is the single largest pipeline opportunity, sourced this way | 🥇 Highest-leverage: one relationship unlocks thousands of hectares at once |
| **Direct to cooperatives/associations** | Coplacana (a cooperative) is in pipeline | 🥇 Same logic — one institutional relationship, many member-producers |
| **Direct to mid-size farms** | Fabio (CAR/SICAR survey client) | 🥈 Works, but one relationship = one property; less efficient per sales hour than the channel/consultant motion above |
| **Financial institutions (banks, credit desks)** | Not yet active — but this is the natural inbound channel once the Passaporte de Crédito Rural is validated as accepted credit evidence (see `09-product/PRODUCT_OVERVIEW.md`) | 🔜 Strategic priority for post-raise: a bank that accepts the Passaporte becomes a demand-generation channel, not just a customer |

## Which comes first, and why

Given the confirmed pipeline, the consultant/cooperative channel
(Thales, Coplacana) is already outperforming direct-to-farm in hectares-
per-relationship. The GTM strategy should lean into this: **find more
Thales-shaped relationships** — independent agronomic consultants and
cooperatives who already have standing relationships across thousands of
hectares — rather than building a farm-by-farm direct sales motion from
scratch.

## Urgency lever — the regulatory driver is now the strongest one

As of March 2026, Banco Central's **Resolução CMN nº 5.267/2025**
requires rural-credit compliance to be proven by remote sensing, and
makes the liability non-delegable — see `06-customers/MARKET_RESEARCH.md`
for the full framing. This changes the sales conversation with banks and
credit desks from "here's a nice-to-have data product" to "you now have
a standing legal obligation and need a vendor who can produce an
auditable, ICP-Brasil-signed dossier." Producer-side urgency is still
tied to credit cycles (Plano Safra financing windows) and carbon/ESG
reporting deadlines — but the institutional-buyer urgency (banks,
cooperatives, credit desks) now has a hard regulatory deadline behind
it, which is the stronger lever for the "Vendas institucionais" GTM
motion this round explicitly funds (30% of the raise — see
`04-investment-documents/USE_OF_FUNDS.md`).
