## Why a flat R$/ha/year subscription, not tiered per-hectare bands

The prior services model (`knowledge-base/PRICING_MODEL.md`) used
declining per-hectare bands by area size (smaller properties pay more
per ha) because each mission had a real marginal cost (pilot time,
fuel, logistics) that didn't scale down cleanly. A **subscription**
model changes that economics: once a property is in the system, the
marginal cost of continuing to serve it is mostly data/compute, not a
repeated physical mission — which is why a flat rate is viable and why
gross margins on the subscription (80% assumed, see
`07-financials/UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`) are structurally higher than the
survey-services side.
