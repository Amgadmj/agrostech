# Hypotheses

**Status: 🟡 structure and logic are sound; specific values are placeholders — see the "Hypotheses" tab in `FINANCIAL_MODEL.xlsx` for the full table with base/bull/bear values and confidence ratings.**

Every number in the financial model traces back to one of 10 named
hypotheses (pricing, survey fee, hectare-growth ramp, pipeline
conversion for Coplacana and Thales specifically, gross margins, churn,
and hiring timing). They're kept in the spreadsheet rather than
duplicated here so the model and the documentation can't drift apart —
open `FINANCIAL_MODEL.xlsx` → **Hypotheses** tab.

**The honest state of these hypotheses today:** most are marked "Low
confidence — placeholder" because AgrosTech doesn't yet have a
subscription cohort old enough to measure churn, a paid-acquisition
channel to measure CAC, or confirmed survey-services pricing under the
current business model. That's not a flaw to hide — it's exactly what
"hypotheses... written out so they can be challenged" is for. The two
highest-confidence rows are the ones the founder has actually confirmed:
the R$4/ha/year price point and the named pipeline accounts (Coplacana,
Thales' 30,000 ha in Bahia).
