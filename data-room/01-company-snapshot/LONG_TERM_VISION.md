## The arc

AgrosTech's roadmap has three stages, each building on the credibility of
the one before it:

### Stage 1 — Field evidence (today)
Drone and satellite data collected per property, tied to a CAR number.
Outputs: NDVI maps, boundary/topography accuracy, biomass estimates. Sold
as one-off survey services and as the data backbone of the subscription
product.

### Stage 2 — Passaporte de Crédito Rural (current product)
The same evidence, standardized and continuously updated, packaged as a
credit-grade document: a "passport" that a bank, cooperative, or
certifier can rely on without commissioning its own survey. Priced as a
**R$ 4/ha/year** subscription — the economics work because the marginal
cost of re-checking a property already in the system is far lower than
the cost of the first survey.

### Stage 3 — LIM: Land viability index
[CONFIRM — founder to define LIM's exact scope.] Directionally: an index
that goes beyond a single property's credit passport to score land
viability more broadly — combining the Passaporte's per-property data
with regional and historical context (climate, deforestation risk, yield
history) so that LIM becomes a reference layer usable by banks,
cooperatives, insurers, and carbon-market participants across an entire
territory, not just a single farm.

## Why this sequencing works

- Each stage **reuses the same underlying data pipeline** (drone +
  satellite + CAR-linked registry data) — the cost of stage 2 is
  amortized stage-1 infrastructure, and stage 3 is amortized stage-2
  infrastructure.
- Each stage has an **independent, provable ROI** (the weed-stress case
  study is stage 1's proof; the CAR/SICAR survey is the registry-grade
  proof that underlies stage 2). Investors aren't being asked to fund a
  bet on stage 3 alone — stages 1 and 2 already have paying customers.
- Stage 2 → 3 is the moat: once a meaningful share of hectares in a
  region carry a Passaporte, LIM becomes materially more accurate than
  any single-property surveyor could produce, because it has population-
  level context no competitor without that install base can replicate.
