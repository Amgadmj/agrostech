## Why this matters before the SAFE closes

The SAFE terms panel is explicit: the post-money valuation cap **already
includes the SAFE money**, so any option pool created *after* the SAFE
closes dilutes the founders, not the SAFE holders. The pool needs to be
sized **before** signing.
