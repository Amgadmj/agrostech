## Why this is a clean story for AgrosTech's current product

The core subscription product (Passaporte de Crédito Rural) is built
from **NDVI/satellite + boundary/topography data**, not spraying — so the
most regulatorily intensive part of the drone-services business (MAPA
aerial-application rules) applies mainly to the one-off survey side, not
the core subscription. This is worth stating explicitly to investors:
the recurring-revenue product has a lighter regulatory footprint than
the services side.
