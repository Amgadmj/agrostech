# LGPD & Data Policies

**Status: 🟡 legal basis is straightforward given the business model; formal policy documents are [CONFIRM]/not yet drafted.**

## Why this applies to AgrosTech specifically

The product's core loop — enter a CAR number, get back geospatial and
credit-relevant data tied to a specific property and, by extension, its
owner — involves personal and property data under LGPD (Lei Geral de
Proteção de Dados). Aerial imagery of rural properties also captures
location data that can be sensitive when it reveals patterns about a
specific individual's land use.

## Legal basis for collection

**Contrato de prestação de serviços** — data is collected under the
service contract with each property owner/producer, whether they're
paying for the R$4/ha/year subscription or a one-off survey.
