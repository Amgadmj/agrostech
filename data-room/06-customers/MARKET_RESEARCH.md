## Total planted area (Brazil)

- IBGE's PAM 2025 (Produção Agrícola Municipal, all 70 tracked crops):
  **101.3 million hectares** planted nationally.
  [IBGE](https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Producao_Agricola/Levantamento_Sistematico_da_Producao_Agricola_%5Bmensal%5D/Fasciculo_Indicadores_IBGE/2025/estProdAgri_202508.pdf)
- IBGE's 2026 estimate for cereals, legumes and oilseeds specifically
  (a narrower slice — doesn't include cana, café, and other perennials
  in the 101.3M figure): **83.1 million hectares**, up 1.8% y/y.
  [Jornal de Brasília](https://jornaldebrasilia.com.br/noticias/economia/safra-2026-alcancara-353-milhoes-de-t-2-maior-que-a-de-2025-afirma-ibge/)

## Rural credit volume (the market the Passaporte de Crédito Rural plugs into)

- **Plano Safra 2025/26** made available **R$ 516.2 billion** for
  business agriculture (R$ 414.7bn financing/commercialization + R$
  101.5bn investment).
  [Grupo Amanhã](https://amanha.com.br/categoria/brasil/plano-safra-2025-2026-disponibilizara-r-516-2-bilhoes)
- **Plano Safra 2026/27** (the coming cycle) expands this to **R$ 525.1
  billion**.
  [O Especialista / Safra](https://oespecialista.safra.com.br/plano-safra-2026-2027-credito-rural-525-bilhoes/)
- Actual contracting through the 2025/26 cycle reached **R$ 404 billion**
  by March 2026 (10% y/y growth), showing this credit is not just
  allocated on paper — it's being drawn down.
  [SouNews](https://sounews.com.br/credito-rural-empresarial-atinge-r-404-bilhoes-no-plano-safra-2025-2026/)

**Why this matters for AgrosTech:** every one of those hundreds of
billions of reais in rural credit requires the lender to assess the
underlying land — boundary accuracy, environmental compliance, and
increasingly, carbon/ESG standing. That assessment is exactly what the
Passaporte de Crédito Rural is built to standardize and continuously
supply.
