## The stated differentiation

> ("Many look from space. We bring the proof down to the ground.")

AgrosTech's claimed edge: **"Satélite, radar SAR e drone por anomalia,
fechados em um dossiê assinado. A evidência que a nova regra pede."**
(Satellite, SAR radar, and drone-on-anomaly, closed into a signed
dossier — the evidence the new rule requires.)

Concretely, this maps to the three proprietary models described in
`09-product/TECH_STACK_OVERVIEW.md`:
- **M1 (Filtro de Área Útil)** and the satellite/registry layer are
  where AgrosTech's offering likely overlaps most with the named
  competitors above (all five appear to be registry+satellite plays).
- **M2 (Radar SAR Anti-Fuga)** and **M3 (Protocolo Halo)**, plus the
  drone-on-anomaly model and the ICP-Brasil-signed MCR 2-9 dossier
  format, are the parts of the claim that need independent verification
  against what these five competitors actually ship — this data room
  has not done that verification.
