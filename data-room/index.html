SAFE term sheet

Based on the YC post-money SAFE (valuation cap, no discount). Download the official form from ycombinator.com/documents and fill only the blanks below. Don't edit the body text; investors trust it because it's unmodified. Full detail: 04-investment-documents/SAFE_TERMS.md

FormPost-Money SAFE, Valuation Cap, no DiscountThe most common version. Skip the MFN-only and discount-only forms.
IssuerAgrosTech's Delaware C-corporationIt must be incorporated, and it must own the Brazilian operating company, before anyone signs.
Round sizeR$3M total across all SAFEsThe SAFE is written in US dollars. Fix the USD amount at the exchange rate on signing day.
Post-money valuation cap≈ R$20M (R$3M ÷ 15%)Post-money means the cap already includes the SAFE money. Any option pool created later dilutes the founders, not the SAFE holders. Size the pool now.
Pro rata rightsSeparate side letter, only for the lead investorGives them the right to keep their percentage in the Series A.
