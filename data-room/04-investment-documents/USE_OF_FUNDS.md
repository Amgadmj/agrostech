hectares à vista até 2028"** — put 1.5 million hectares "in sight"

(M1 Filtro de Área Útil, M2 Radar SAR Anti-Fuga, M3 Protocolo Halo — see

strategically important: it's what turns the product from "useful data"
into **compliance infrastructure directly serving Resolução CMN
5.267/2025** — see `06-customers/MARKET_RESEARCH.md`.
