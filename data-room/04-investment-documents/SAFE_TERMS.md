# SAFE Term Sheet

**Status: ✅ terms confirmed by founder — Issuer field blocked on `03-company-documents/INCORPORATION_STATUS.md`.**

Based on the YC post-money SAFE (valuation cap, no discount). Download
the official form from [ycombinator.com/documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents)
and fill only the blanks below — don't edit the body text; investors
trust it because it's unmodified.

| Term | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Form** | Post-Money SAFE, Valuation Cap, no Discount | The most common version. Skip the MFN-only and discount-only forms. |
| **Issuer** | 🔴 **BLOCKED** — AgrosTech's Delaware C-corporation | Must be incorporated, and must own the Brazilian operating company, before anyone signs. **Neither exists yet** — see `03-company-documents/INCORPORATION_STATUS.md`. A Brazil-only instrument is an open alternative worth deciding with counsel before defaulting into a Delaware flip. |
| **Round size** | R$ 3,000,000 total across all SAFEs | The SAFE is written in US dollars. Fix the USD amount at the exchange rate on signing day. |
| **Post-money valuation cap** | ≈ R$ 20,000,000 (R$3M ÷ 15%) | Post-money means the cap already includes the SAFE money. Any option pool created later dilutes the founders, not the SAFE holders. Size the pool now — see `02-team/VESTING_AND_ESOP.md`. |
| **Pro rata rights** | Separate side letter, only for the lead investor | Gives them the right to keep their percentage in the Series A. |
| **Governing law** | Delaware | Already in the form. **Contingent on the Delaware-flip decision above** — if a Brazilian instrument is used instead, this changes. |
| **Company signs** | Amgad, as CEO, after a board consent approving the SAFE issuance | See `03-company-documents/BOARD_CONSENTS.md`. |

## Before signing (unmodified from the source checklist)

Have a Brazilian lawyer and a US lawyer review the Delaware–Brazil
structure, the currency flow (*registro no Banco Central*), and tax.
This is a planning checklist, not legal advice.

## What has to happen first (sequencing, not just legal review)

1. Resolve the entity structure question (`INCORPORATION_STATUS.md`).
2. Size and board-approve the option pool
   (`02-team/VESTING_AND_ESOP.md`, `03-company-documents/BOARD_CONSENTS.md`).
3. Board consent approving the SAFE issuance itself.
4. Only then: fill in the Issuer field on the actual YC form and sign.
